Step 4: Start-up options

1. After restarting your computer, choose " Try or Install Ubuntu ", and wait for the Ubuntu OS to boot up.

2. Next is to choose your accessibilty options.

Seeing
  • High Contrast: Increases the color distinction between text, icons, and backgrounds to make elements easier to identify.
  • Large Text: Enlarges the system font size for better readability.
  • Reduce Animation: Minimizes sliding or fading effects to help users sensitive to motion.
  • Screen Reader: Provides synthesized voice output of on-screen text and interface elements.

Hearing
  • Visual Alerts: Flashes the screen or a specific window when a system alert sound triggers.

Typing
  • Sticky Keys: Allows the use of modifier keys (like Shift or Ctrl) by pressing them one at a time instead of holding them down simultaneously.
  • Slow Keys: Sets a delay between a key being pressed and the character appearing, helping to prevent accidental keystrokes..

Pointing and Clicking
  • Mouse Keys: Enables the use of the numeric keypad on your keyboard to control the mouse pointer.

Zoom
  • Desktop Zoom: Magnifies specific areas of the screen to provide a closer view of the interface.

3. After partitioning, keep your flash drive plugged in , and simply Restart your computer.
